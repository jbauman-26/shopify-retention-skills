# Campaign calendar planner

## What you need
- `brand-voice.md` and `segments.md` if available
- Product catalog, upcoming launches, key dates, how many sends a week the team can handle

## Steps
1. Set the mix: at most one in three sends is a promotion. The rest teach, tell a story or show proof.
2. For each week, plan the sends with: date, segment, type, one-line idea, the product it supports.
3. Pull education and proof ideas from reviews and support tickets (questions customers keep asking are campaigns).
4. Write a 5-line brief per send: goal, reader, one idea, proof to use, the single link.
5. Mark which sends VIPs get early and which lapsed customers skip.

## Output
`campaign-calendar.md` with a calendar table and the briefs.

## Rules that apply to every output

- Never invent customer quotes, results or numbers. If the data is missing, say so.
- Strip names, emails, phone numbers and order numbers from anything you quote.
- Everything you create in Klaviyo stays a draft. A person switches it on.
- Write like one person talking to one customer. Plain words, one idea per email, one link.
