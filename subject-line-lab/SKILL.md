# Subject line lab

## What you need
- The email draft or brief
- `brand-voice.md`, and review phrases if available
- The segment size and the brand's usual click rate

## Steps
1. Write 10 subject lines in five styles: customer phrase, plain statement, a question the reader is already asking, specific number, curiosity with a clear payoff. Under 45 characters where possible.
2. Write preview text for the best three. It finishes the thought and never repeats the subject.
3. Cut anything that reads like a promo to someone who isn't being promoted to: all caps, "don't miss", fake urgency, "RE:".
4. **Testing.** Estimate clicks per version: segment size x share receiving that version x usual click rate.
   - **About 200 or more clicks per version:** a single-send A/B test can tell you something, but only about big differences (roughly 30% or more between versions). Smaller differences need far more clicks. Split the send in two and compare click rate.
   - **Fewer than that (most brands under about 50,000 subscribers):** one send can't pick a real winner, even though the tool will name one. Instead, pick two styles and alternate them across the next 6 to 8 sends, then compare total clicks per recipient for each style.
5. Judge on click rate and placed-order rate. Open rate is unreliable.

## Output
The 10 lines grouped by style, the top three with preview text, the estimated clicks per version, and the matching test plan.

## Rules that apply to every output

- Never invent customer quotes, results or numbers. If the data is missing, say so.
- Strip names, emails, phone numbers and order numbers from anything you quote.
- Everything you create in Klaviyo stays a draft. A person switches it on.
- Write like one person talking to one customer. Plain words, one idea per email, one link.
