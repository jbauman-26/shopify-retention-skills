# SMS flow companion

## What you need
- The email flows already written
- Confirmation that the brand collects **prior express written consent** for marketing texts, and which tool sends them (Klaviyo SMS, Postscript, Attentive)

## Steps
1. For each flow, pick at most one point where a text helps more than an email: checkout recovery, delivery day, or a reorder reminder.
2. **Abandoned cart: one text per abandoned cart, sent within 48 hours.** That is the carrier (CTIA) rule. Never two.
3. Write each message under 160 characters, brand name first, one link.
4. Include opt-out wording ("Reply STOP to opt out") where the tool or the law requires it.
5. **Quiet hours:** US federal rules allow texts from 8am to 9pm in the recipient's local time. Several states are stricter. Turn on the sending tool's quiet hours and default to 9am to 8pm local unless the brand's counsel says otherwise.
6. Never text people who haven't opted in to SMS. Where the brand uses double opt-in, only text people who completed it. If consent status is unclear, stop and ask.
7. A text never repeats the email. It adds one new thing.

## Output
`sms-companion.md` with each message, where it sits in the flow, and the consent and quiet-hours checks.

This isn't legal advice. Texting rules vary by state and change. The brand confirms its own compliance.

## Rules that apply to every output

- Never invent customer quotes, results or numbers. If the data is missing, say so.
- Strip names, emails, phone numbers and order numbers from anything you quote.
- Everything you create in Klaviyo stays a draft. A person switches it on.
- Write like one person talking to one customer. Plain words, one idea per email, one link.
